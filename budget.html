Why is October higher?

The current schedule is every other Friday, with Jason paydays on October 2, 16 and 30. That adds one normal $3,522.74 take-home check in October; it is not a raise or bonus.

The eight-check plan is distributed 1 in September, 3 in October, 2 in November and 2 in December. Earlier discussion described two checks per month, but the saved schedule is biweekly. Payroll dates have not been changed. If we change that timing, this projection must change too.
