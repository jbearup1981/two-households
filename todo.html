Next steps

A short sequence for keeping the household plan current.

First: replace the unknowns

  1. Reconcile bill-pay cash and remaining September bills.
  2. Confirm card minimums, due dates and scheduled debits.
  3. Verify first payroll is ready and funded before counting the deposit.
  4. Add maintenance, holiday and irregular costs once identified.

Then: protect the cushion

  • Follow the working $1,200 monthly spending reductions.
  • Keep October’s extra-check remainder for later obligations.
  • Retain apartment rent until written exit terms are confirmed.
  • Review the projection whenever payroll dates or income change.

Before year-end

  • Verify 2025 return status and the existing IRS payment arrangement.
  • Recalculate 2026 taxes using corrected business figures and both spouses’ withholding.
  • Identify actual January catch-up cash and preserve essential bill money.
  • Set the 2027 salary and budget separately.

This is a maintained plan, not an automated reminder or payment schedule.
