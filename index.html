The tax situation, in plain English

The business is an S-corp. That is a tax setup, not a different company — same Nexus, filed differently since January 1.

The business itself pays no income tax. Its profit passes through onto our personal return, so there is no such thing as "the business's taxes" separate from ours. It is one bill.

What the S-corp does require is that Jason pay himself an actual paycheck rather than just taking money out. Money he takes beyond that paycheck comes out as a distribution, which skips the 15.3% Social Security and Medicare hit. That split is the whole reason for the S-corp and it saves several thousand dollars a year.

Behind us — 2025

We filed an extension, so nothing is late. The 2025 return simply is not finished yet; it gets done in the next few months. There is a balance owed to the IRS right now — probably a couple of thousand — and finishing 2025 will add to it. That combined number is exactly what the $500 a month is paying down. It is known, contained, and already being handled, not a surprise waiting to land.

Carried on this page at $20,000 to stay on the safe side until the return is done. The real figure is likely lower — worth pinning down, because it decides whether the house proceeds clear it outright.
